SYNTHETIC TEST AGREEMENT — NOT A CLIENT DOCUMENT
Harbor Point Example LLC is owned by Alpha Member and Beta Member. This short fictional agreement exists only to test the review application.

Section 1. Capital calls. The Manager may issue a written capital call only for expenditures in an approved budget. Alpha Member and Beta Member must contribute equally within 15 business days after receipt of the call. If a Member does not contribute when due, the Manager must send written notice of the shortfall. The Member has 10 business days after receipt of that notice to cure. After that cure period expires without full payment, the other Member may elect to advance the shortfall as a loan to the Company. Such an advance does not change either Member's percentage ownership or voting rights.

Section 2. Major decisions. A sale of all Company assets, admission of a new Member, or amendment of this Agreement requires written approval of both Members. If the Members disagree on a major decision, their principals must meet within 10 business days after either Member requests a meeting in writing. If no agreement is reached within 20 business days after that meeting, the matter remains unapproved. The Manager must continue operations within the existing approved budget. This section grants no buy-sell or dissolution right.

Section 3. Manager removal. Beta Member is the Manager. Alpha Member may remove the Manager for a material breach of this Agreement only after giving written notice identifying the breach and allowing 30 calendar days to cure. A breach cured within that period does not support removal under this section. Removal does not cancel Beta Member's ownership interest or accrued fees. Appointment of a replacement Manager requires both Members' written approval.

Section 4. Transfers. Neither Member may transfer its ownership interest without the other Member's prior written consent. A transfer to an entity wholly owned by the transferring Member is permitted without that consent if the transferee first signs a written assumption of this Agreement and the transferring Member remains liable for its obligations. Transfers of Company assets are governed by Section 2. No right of first refusal is granted by this section.
