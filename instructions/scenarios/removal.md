# Operator removal / default analysis — version 1
Identify actual management roles, removal grounds and whether removal can occur without cause. For each applicable ground, trace the required proof, notice, cure and extensions, decision-maker and effect of disputes. Distinguish alleged misconduct from contractual requirements for establishing it. Identify replacement qualifications, appointment, acceptance, lender/third-party approvals, transition duties and exact effective time.
Trace economic consequences: future/accrued fees, promote or carried interest, capital, preferred return, ownership and voting. Identify retained entitlements, offsets, forfeiture, clawback and indemnity only as supported. Do not assume management removal transfers ownership or establishes damages. Flag missing factual records, financing documents or interpretive questions and preserve ground-specific differences.
