# Funding default analysis — version 1
Trace a member's possible failure to contribute additional capital under this agreement. Establish who may call capital, for what purposes, approval requirements, allocation, amount/detail, notice delivery and minimum lead time. Separate a shortfall, default notice, cure expiry and any defined Event of Default. Identify exact cure triggers, repayment alternatives and whether different default grounds have different cures.
Identify all express funding remedies and their conditions: loans, interest, priority, dilution, buyout, voting restrictions, damages or other mechanisms only if actually supported. Determine the borrower, lender, discretionary/mandatory nature and economic consequences. Trace distribution priorities, financing restrictions and surviving reserved approvals. Distinguish loan advance from repayment/cure and ownership changes from governance changes. If removal is available, map its separate conditions rather than assuming it follows automatically. Flag missing calls, approvals, receipts, payment records, financing documents and disputed predicates. Do not use illustrative numeric terms from the prototype.
