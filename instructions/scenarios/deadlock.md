# Major-decision deadlock analysis — version 1
Determine the actual approval structure, reserved decisions, delegated ordinary authority and any casting vote. Identify what qualifies as deadlock: proposal completeness, meetings, refusals, timing and notice. Trace escalation, senior representatives, mediation/arbitration or other mechanisms, distinguishing optional and mandatory steps. Identify permitted interim operations and spending while disagreement persists.
Map any exit trigger including lock-outs, elapsed time, election mechanics, valuation, broker/appraiser selection, buy-sell, sale or dissolution, but only as stated. Do not assume deadlock equals default or removal. Distinguish a process election from completed sale/dissolution. Identify price/marketing/consent gates and what happens if the process fails. If a fallback is absent or unclear, pose an open question rather than inventing one. Preserve relevant lender and dispute-resolution conditions.
