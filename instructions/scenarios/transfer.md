# Transfer / exit analysis — version 1
Identify direct and indirect transfers, changes of control, consent rules, lock-up, permitted transfers and conditions for Affiliate status. Distinguish a defined Affiliate from any narrower permitted-transferee qualification. Trace notice, joinder, continuing liability, eligibility and lender consent.
Identify any ROFO, ROFR, tag, drag, buy-sell, buyout or other exit mechanism using its actual structure, not its label alone. Trace acceptance, contracting and closing windows, price/non-price restrictions, reset conditions and exceptions. Do not import a matching right into a first-offer procedure. Distinguish membership-interest transfer from an asset sale, dissolution or manager replacement. Identify unauthorized-transfer consequences and cure/unwind procedures. Flag missing ownership records, offers, sale agreements and financing information.
